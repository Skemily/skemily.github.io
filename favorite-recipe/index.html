Bacon-Wrapped Water Chestnuts

The perfect appetizer for your Super Bowl party! These sweet and savory bites are always the first gone.

Ingredients

  • Roughly 1 pound of bacon, cut in half
  • 1 can whole water chestnuts

For the sauce:

  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

Directions

For the bacon wrapped chestnuts

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees.
  2. Wrap each water chestnut in bacon and secure with a wooden toothpick.
  3. Place bacon-wrapped water chestnuts on a large rimmed baking tray.
  4. Bake for 20 minutes.
  5. Remove from oven and carefully flip bacon-wrapped water chestnuts with tongs.
  6. Bake for another 15-20 minutes or until bacon is just crispy.
  7. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes.

For the sauce

  1. Whisk ketchup and brown sugar in a small sauce pan on low heat until the brown sugar melts and sauce begins to bubble, stirring freqently.
  2. Add Worcestershire sauce. Stir and taste. Add more if desired.
  3. Transfer wrapped chestnuts onto a serving platter and pour warm sauce over the top to coat them.
  4. Serve warm or at room temperature.
